About the Salon

A salon that listens.

At Salon Scarpello, great hair starts with a real conversation. Our stylists take the time to understand what you want, offer honest suggestions, and deliver results you’ll love walking out with.

Our space is clean, open and vibrant, with fresh flowers, a little sparkle, and a friendly team that makes everyone feel at home, from first-time guests to regulars and little ones.
